# GPT-Image-2 Logo Brief

Date: 2026-07-27

## Current status

The final logo was generated on July 27, 2026 through the ChatGPT images surface in Chrome after direct API access remained blocked on the machine network.

## Brand direction

- Brand: `PhotoReady`
- Parent company: `Shanmu Software`
- Tone: clear, practical, modern, trustworthy
- Audience: global job seekers, travelers, visa applicants, and self-serve buyers

## Prompt draft

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: website logo for a web-first B2C product
Primary request: create a clean, modern logo for "PhotoReady", a tool that turns one portrait into passport, LinkedIn, and resume-ready photo exports
Scene/backdrop: flat neutral background
Subject: a logo system combining a simple symbol and the PhotoReady wordmark
Style/medium: vector-friendly brand mark, minimal, professional, not playful
Composition/framing: centered logo lockup with one square app-style symbol and one horizontal wordmark version
Lighting/mood: crisp, confident, lightweight
Color palette: deep navy, soft green accent, warm sand neutral
Text (verbatim): "PhotoReady"
Constraints: no camera icon cliches, no sparkles, no mascot, no fake 3D effects, no gradients that reduce readability
Avoid: stock AI aesthetics, medical look, corporate enterprise heaviness
```
