# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-07-27

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** PhotoReady turns one portrait into job-search and document-ready exports for global users, while Shanmu Software sells a small shelf of operations-focused digital packs on the same site.

**What it does:** PhotoReady is a web-first B2C tool for people who need a clean passport photo, LinkedIn headshot, and resume portrait without visiting a studio or learning editing software. The same site also sells PFMEA, lean, and office operations packs as standardized side products.

**Product category:** Online ID photo tool, job application photo tool, digital productivity packs.

**Product type:** Web app plus digital download catalog.

**Business model:** Free preview, one-time export purchase, subscription for repeat use, and one-time digital pack sales.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Not company-led in the main motion. Primary buyers are individuals; secondary buyers are small engineering and operations teams buying digital packs.

**Decision-makers:** Individual job seekers, travelers, visa applicants, freelancers, and self-serve engineering or quality managers.

**Primary use case:** Turn one ordinary portrait into a usable passport, visa, LinkedIn, or resume photo quickly.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Create a document-compliant photo without visiting a photo shop.
- Refresh a professional profile image for job applications and networking.
- Buy ready-made PFMEA and lean templates instead of commissioning custom B2B work.

**Use cases:**
- Overseas visa and passport photo preparation
- LinkedIn profile refresh
- Resume/CV application kits
- PFMEA offline analysis pack purchase
- Lean checklist and office operations template purchase

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** People need official or professional-looking photos quickly, but current options are slow, unclear, or require manual editing skill.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Studio visits cost time and money.
- Generic photo editors do not explain target output use cases.
- Many B2B engineering tools require demos, calls, or custom setup.

**What it costs them:** Delayed applications, extra editing time, repeated formatting work, and lower confidence in the final result.

**Emotional tension:** Urgency, uncertainty, and frustration right before a deadline-driven application or trip.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Online passport photo and AI headshot tools — often narrow, expensive, or generic.

**Secondary:** Canva, Photoshop, phone editing apps — flexible but require manual work and confidence.

**Indirect:** Local photo studios and print shops — reliable but slow, offline, and inconvenient.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- One upload supports multiple outcomes on one site.
- Job-search and official-photo use cases live together.
- Side-product catalog creates extra monetization without distracting from the main flow.
- Built and iterated quickly with Codex, which keeps scope small and shipping fast.

**How we do it differently:** We sell clear outcomes, not a vague AI promise. The site focuses on three first-launch presets and simple buying choices.

**Why that's better:** Visitors understand what they get quickly, which reduces friction and improves first-session conversion.

**Why customers choose us:** Faster than a studio, simpler than a full photo editor, and more practical than a general AI image site for this specific job.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Will this photo actually look professional?" | The site is focused on profile, resume, and document-ready exports with preset framing and background rules. |
| "Why should I pay instead of editing it myself?" | The product removes manual sizing, cleanup, and export work in one flow. |
| "What if I only need this once?" | There is a one-time export pack designed for one-off buyers. |

**Anti-persona:** Teams looking for large-scale enterprise workflow customization, consulting-heavy deployments, or deep industrial integration.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Studio inconvenience, confusing DIY tools, and slow B2B buying motions.

**Pull:** Faster output, easier presets, English-first landing pages, and dollar-denominated self-serve checkout.

**Habit:** People are used to either doing it manually or postponing the task.

**Anxiety:** Worry that the output will not be accepted or will look low quality.
